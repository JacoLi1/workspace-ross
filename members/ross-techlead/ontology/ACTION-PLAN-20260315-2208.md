   - Class vs Property hierarchies
   - ObjectProperty, DatatypeProperty, AnnotationProperty
   - Restrictions (some, only, min/max cardinality)

2. ** kitchenDevice 分类学设计**
   - Functional classes: `CookingDevice` (加热设备), `PrepDevice` (处理设备), `StorageDevice` (存储)
   - Physical properties: `hasTemperatureRange`, `hasPowerSource`, `hasCapacity`
   - Operational states: `isOn`, `currentTemperature`, `operationalStatus`

3. **Protégé 工具熟练度** (optional but helpful)

:Stove a owl:Class ; rdfs:subClassOf :KitchenDevice .
